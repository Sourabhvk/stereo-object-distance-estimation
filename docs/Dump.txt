Stereo Vision Notes

Baseline:
The baseline is the physical distance between the left and right cameras.

For KITTI-style stereo images, the baseline is approximately:
baseline_m = 0.54 meters

Depth formula:
depth = focal_length * baseline / disparity

In this project:
depth_m = focal_length_px * baseline_m / disparity_px

Example calibration values:
focal_length_px = 721.5377
baseline_m = 0.54

Meaning:
- focal_length_px is the camera focal length measured in pixels.
- baseline_m is the distance between the stereo cameras in meters.
- disparity_px is how far the same point shifts between the left and right images.
- depth_m is the estimated distance from the camera in meters.
